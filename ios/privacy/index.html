EgoMetra

Privacy policy

Updated: 22 October 2026

Developer and data controller: Viktor Palej, Lithuania. Privacy and support contact: egometra.support@gmail.com

Microphone and audio

EgoMetra uses the microphone only after you start the tuner or analysis and grant permission. Pitch detection and intonation analysis run on your device; audio is not uploaded to our servers. The tuner does not save audio. Analysis can save the recorded phrase locally for playback. When the app is hidden or the screen is locked, microphone capture stops; a phrase already played is saved if usable notes were detected. Returning to the app does not restart the microphone automatically.

Local data and retention

Practice programs, settings, language preferences, analysis measurements and recordings are stored on this device. No EgoMetra account is required. Programs and settings remain until you delete them or clear app/browser data. Up to 30 analyses are retained; adding a new analysis removes the oldest excess result and its audio. Local data is not automatically synchronised to our servers.

Deleting your data

In Analysis, Delete removes the selected result and its saved audio; Clear all removes the saved history and recordings. If audio deletion fails, EgoMetra reports the error and keeps the result so you can retry. On iPhone and iPad, Delete App removes the app and its local data. Offload App keeps its documents and data, and removing the icon from the Home Screen does not delete the app. Exported files, copies you sent elsewhere and existing device backups must be managed separately.

Transfers and third-party services

Program export and sharing happen only when you choose them. The iPhone and iPad app contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not sell your data. Capacitor provides the iOS runtime; it does not send your recordings to us. Opening an external service is governed by that service's privacy policy. When you use TestFlight, Apple provides us with crash logs, usage statistics and feedback, which may be linked to your tester identity. We use these details to diagnose issues, improve EgoMetra and handle feedback, and retain them only as long as needed for testing, support or legal obligations. Apple operates TestFlight under its own terms and privacy policy.

Email support

Write to us opens your email app. You choose the sending account, review the message and send it yourself; you may also copy our address. We receive your sender address, message and any attachments you send. We use them to respond and resolve the request, based on our legitimate interest in providing support. We retain correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request and related issues or meet legal obligations. Email providers process messages under their own privacy policies.

Children and parents

EgoMetra is intended for musicians aged 9 and above, including children and adults. Microphone audio, recordings and analysis remain on the device regardless of age. Parents can manage microphone permission in device settings and delete local recordings in Analysis. Children under 13 should ask a parent or guardian to contact support on their behalf. If we learn that a support sender is under 13, we use their contact details only for a one-time response and promptly delete their contact information and correspondence afterwards; we do not retain them for further contact. Further support should be handled through a parent or guardian.

Security

Local data relies on the protections of your iPhone or iPad. Depending on your Apple device and backup settings, app data may be included in device backups, including iCloud backups. Apple handles these copies under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or control your device backups and cannot restore or delete them for you. Deleting data in EgoMetra does not remove existing backup copies. Email is handled by email providers; avoid including unnecessary sensitive information.

Your choices and rights

You can revoke microphone permission in device settings and delete local data as described above. For personal information in support correspondence, contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability where applicable. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority, including Lithuania’s State Data Protection Inspectorate. We cannot access recordings that remain only on your device.

Changes to this policy

This policy describes the current EgoMetra functionality. If data handling changes, we will update this document and its date. Any new use that requires your permission will be explained before it starts.

Email service privacy policy

When this policy is published on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes under its own privacy statement. The policy page does not upload microphone audio or local EgoMetra recordings. Reading the policy inside the app does not require visiting the hosted website.
